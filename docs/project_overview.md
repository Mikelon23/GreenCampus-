# Project Overview

GreenCampus+ Platform

## 1. Introduction

GreenCampus+ is a sustainability intelligence platform designed for university campuses.
Its purpose is to help universities monitor environmental conditions, analyze sustainability metrics, and encourage students and staff to participate in eco-friendly initiatives.

The platform integrates environmental data monitoring, sustainability analytics, and community engagement tools into a single digital ecosystem.

GreenCampus+ aims to transform campuses into living laboratories where environmental awareness, data-driven decision making, and student innovation work together to promote sustainability.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Universities are increasingly committed to sustainability goals, but many campuses lack integrated digital systems capable of:

* monitoring environmental indicators
* engaging students in sustainability initiatives
* measuring environmental impact
* promoting collaborative innovation for climate solutions

Existing tools are often fragmented and do not provide a unified view of sustainability performance.

GreenCampus+ addresses this challenge by providing a centralized platform that combines environmental monitoring, analytics, and community participation.

---

## 3. Platform Objectives

The main objectives of the platform are:

1. Monitor environmental indicators across the campus.
2. Provide sustainability analytics through dashboards.
3. Track carbon footprint indicators.
4. Encourage eco-friendly behaviors through gamification.
5. Facilitate student innovation through sustainability hackathons.

---

## 4. Target Users

The platform is designed for three primary user groups.

### Students

Students can:

* track their sustainability activities
* earn eco-points
* participate in environmental challenges
* join sustainability hackathons

### Administrators

University administrators can:

* monitor environmental metrics
* evaluate sustainability indicators
* manage campus initiatives

### Researchers and Sustainability Teams

Researchers can:

* analyze environmental data
* evaluate sustainability performance
* generate reports and insights.

---

## 5. Core Features

The GreenCampus+ platform includes several key functional modules.

### Environmental Monitoring

The system collects and visualizes environmental indicators such as:

* temperature
* humidity
* CO₂ levels
* energy consumption

These data may come from sensors or simulated environmental models.

---

### Sustainability Dashboard

The dashboard provides visual analytics including:

* environmental trends
* sustainability scores
* energy consumption patterns
* carbon impact indicators

---

### Carbon Footprint Tracker

Users can track personal or institutional carbon footprint indicators based on their activities and resource consumption.

---

### Gamification System

Students are encouraged to participate in sustainability initiatives through:

* green points
* badges
* leaderboard rankings

Gamification promotes behavioral change and environmental awareness.

---

### GreenHack Hub

The GreenHack Hub is an innovation module where students can:

* participate in sustainability challenges
* form teams
* submit projects
* track environmental impact

This module promotes student-driven solutions to environmental problems.

---

## 6. Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

The initial version of the platform will include:

* Environmental monitoring dashboard
* Simulated sensor data
* Carbon footprint tracking
* Green points and leaderboard
* Basic hackathon event system

The MVP focuses on demonstrating the platform’s potential and core capabilities.

---

## 7. Expected Impact

GreenCampus+ aims to:

* improve environmental awareness among students
* provide data-driven sustainability insights
* promote eco-friendly behavior
* support innovation for climate solutions

The platform transforms sustainability from a policy concept into an interactive digital experience.
